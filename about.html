About

From small robots to systems that evaluate AI systems.

I am an AI engineer and technical educator based in India. I currently lead AI engineering work at Weights & Biases (part of CoreWeave), focused on coding agents, evaluation harnesses, memory, and improvement loops for agentic systems.

The short version of my work is: build the system, measure it honestly, and teach what survives contact with reality.

How I work

I am drawn to the part of AI engineering that is difficult to see in a demo: instrumentation, datasets, failure analysis, evaluation criteria, and the feedback loops that make a system improve. I enjoy moving between implementation and explanation—building a tool, stress-testing it, and then writing or teaching the reusable idea behind it.

That pattern connects most of my work: integrations that expose what a model is doing, evaluations that replace intuition with evidence, and courses that turn months of engineering lessons into a path someone else can follow.

The “Enginear” story

When I was ten, a teacher asked what I wanted to become. I said “engineer.” Asked to spell it, I got it wrong, and was told that someone who could not spell the word could not become one.

I kept the misspelling—Enginear—as a reminder that an early mistake is not a verdict. Curiosity, practice, and the willingness to keep building matter much more.
